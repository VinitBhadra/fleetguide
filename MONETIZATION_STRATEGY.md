# FleetGuide.in Monetization Strategy

This document outlines a comprehensive monetization strategy for FleetGuide.in, focusing on Google AdSense as the primary revenue source, with additional opportunities for growth.

#### 2. GPS Guides Page

#### 3. Transport Rules Page

## Phase 1: AdSense Optimization (Months 1-3)

### Week 1-2: Setup & Verification
- ✅ AdSense account created
- ✅ Ads implemented across all pages
- ✅ Privacy policy added
- ✅ Cookie consent banner installed
- Monitor ad serving and fix any issues

### Week 3-4: Initial Optimization
- Monitor AdSense dashboard daily
- Track key metrics:
  - Page RPM
  - CTR (Click-through rate)
  - Coverage (ad requests filled)
  - CPC (Cost per click)
- Identify top-performing pages
- Adjust underperforming placements

### Month 2-3: A/B Testing
Test different ad configurations:

**Test 1: Ad Density**
- Variant A: Current setup (5-6 ads per page)
- Variant B: Reduced ads (3-4 per page)
- Measure: User engagement vs. revenue

**Test 2: Ad Formats**
- Test: Display ads vs. In-feed vs. In-article
- Measure: CTR and revenue per format

**Test 3: Ad Positions**
- Test: Above fold vs. Mid-content vs. End of content
- Measure: Viewability and revenue
